How to Play

WASD move   Space jump   Shift sprint

Hold left click mine / attack   Right click place, use, eat

E inventory + crafting   1-9 / scroll hotbar   V 1st / 3rd person   T chat (online)

U use the offhand item (hold U to raise a shield)   M switch Survival / Creative at any time   Y skins menu (Steve / Alex)

Creative: double-tap Space to fly, Space up, Shift down. Instant mining, unlimited blocks, no damage. Open E for every item.

Right-click a Crafting Table to craft with it. Bow: right click to shoot.

Obsidian: place stone or cobblestone on a water block (mine it with a diamond pickaxe). Build a frame of 3 obsidian blocks on the bottom, 3 on the top and 3 on each side (a 3x3 gap, corners not needed; other closed rectangles work too), craft Flint and Steel (1 iron ingot + 1 cobblestone), and right-click the frame to open a Nether portal.

Right-click doors to open / close, chests for loot, villagers with an emerald above their head to trade.

Eye of Ender (12 iron + 5 wood + 2 emeralds): right-click to throw, dig down where it hovers.

Don't hit villagers, iron golems defend them! Hit cows, pigs and chickens for loot.

Model flips if something faces backwards: F player, G animals, H dragon.
